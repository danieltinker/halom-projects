The place

Where the jungle
meets the Pacific

Selva sits on a secluded hillside above Playa Santa Teresa, at the southern tip of the Nicoya Peninsula — one of the world’s Blue Zones. Howler monkeys at breakfast, world‑class surf by noon, and every evening the sun sinks straight into the ocean.
