### Changes made by Halom

The file in `cars/concept.glb` is a modified copy of the original `CarConcept.glb`:

- The Khronos Group logo and the 3D Commerce logo were removed. The licence of the model excludes logos and
  trademarks, so none is shown here: the logo picture on the licence plate and on the steering-wheel emblem,
  the two logos printed on the tyre sidewalls, and the two logos raised in the tyre sidewalls' relief.
- The copyright line inside the file was rewritten to this attribution.
- Parts that never move apart were joined, vertices were quantised and the geometry was compressed (meshopt).
  Large pictures were re-encoded as JPEG. No triangle was removed.
- On the page the paint colours, the glass, the wheel finishes, the tyre rubber and the lamps are set by Halom,
  and the licence plate carries the name of the showroom.
